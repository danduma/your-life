# Your Life

A visualization of your life, as divided into years, months, and weeks.

Novelty: This fork adds several new features to the original visualization:
- Current date input (defaults to today)
- Gender-specific average lifespans (male: 79.4, female: 85.1 years)
- Average age of chronic disease onset (male: 63, female: 62 years)
- Color-coded life phases:
  - Red: Life already lived
  - Green: Healthy life ahead
  - Yellow: Life with chronic conditions
  - Brown: Gender-specific differences (disease onset and lifespan gaps)
- High-resolution PNG export functionality
